# Match the Venue to the Contribution

## Purpose
Submit to the venue whose reviewers will recognize the actual contribution, and avoid late-stage submission friction from missing venue-specific requirements.

## Process
1. Identify the core contribution type: is it primarily domain expertise (a new dataset capturing real domain complexity) or primarily methodological (a new evaluation paradigm, not just a new dataset)? This determines venue fit more than subfield.
2. For domain-expertise-led contributions, consider domain-specific workshops or venues where reviewers have the relevant background to assess data quality and realism.
3. For methodology-led contributions, target general ML/NLP venues with dedicated dataset/benchmark tracks (e.g., NeurIPS Datasets & Benchmarks, ACL benchmark tracks).
4. Check the venue's current call for papers, author instructions, and required artifacts early. Requirements change; verify them from official sources and record when they were checked. Prepare datasheets, checklists, ethics statements, or other materials that the selected venue requests.

## Output
A venue decision with rationale, plus any required submission artifacts (datasheet, reproducibility checklist) drafted alongside the manuscript.

## Pitfalls
- Requirements, tracks, and deadlines change between cycles. Recheck official venue pages for the intended submission year.
- Submitting a methodology-led contribution to a narrow domain workshop (or vice versa) often results in reviewers who can't fully evaluate the actual contribution.
